## 5. 完整示例 prompt（春·廊亭·斜倚栏杆）

```
Spring scene in Jiangnan scholar's garden: a Southern Song lady leans on the wooden rail of a waterside pavilion (langting). She has a low chignon with loose wispy strands and a simple jade hairpin. She wears a sheer silk beizi in oat color over a soft cream inner robe. Her expression is a soft smile gazing into the distance, half-lidded eyes. The foreground has a single lotus leaf partially framing the figure. Behind her, crabapple blossoms are just opening (a few pink petals drifting onto her shoulder). The composition leaves half the frame empty as breathing space. Distant pavilions and a lotus pond appear in soft misty silhouette. The light is soft morning side-back light, casting gentle rim light on her hair. Subtle atmospheric haze softens shadows. Her skin has natural translucency. 85mm portrait lens, low saturation palette of lotus-root pink and pale celadon, subtle film grain, museum-quality composition
```
